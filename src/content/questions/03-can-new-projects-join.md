---
title: Can new projects join PRAGMA?
---

Yes. The Project Committee, the group that reviews incoming project applications, opens applications twice a year. Applications are currently open through the [project intake form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSf1nlUEAbmc1Hb4r_H8knRGQ7p-e6Ihim5zisQRzYnzbwld9w/viewform). The [project path](/projects#project-path) explains the process from application through review, incubation and final acceptance. The Project Committee reviews candidates and recommends to the Administrative Board, the board that handles day-to-day operations, which ones should become PRAGMA projects.
