---
title: Where are PRAGMA decisions recorded?
---

Major decisions are recorded publicly through PRAGMA Decision Records and board materials. A PDR is a public record explaining a governance or operating decision, why it was made and how it should be followed. The main repositories are [pragma-org/PDRs](https://github.com/pragma-org/PDRs) and [pragma-org/board](https://github.com/pragma-org/board).
